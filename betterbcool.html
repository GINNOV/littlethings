Wrist-Temperature Night Cooling

Apple Watch produces aggregated appleSleepingWristTemperature samples during sleep tracking. betterBCool calculates a multi-night baseline and triggers comfort cooling whenever your body temperature elevates beyond your chosen threshold.

  • Samples evaluated for up to 18 hours
  • Requires 3 prior samples before engaging
  • 100% read-only HealthKit permissions

FeverFrida BLE Explorer & Labs

Contains exploratory BLE decoder tools and CoreBluetooth inspectors (FeverFridaKit) built to research continuous wearable temperature sensors (WT701).
