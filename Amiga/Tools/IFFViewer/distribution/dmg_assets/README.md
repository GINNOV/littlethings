# PixDeluxe Quick Look

Requires macOS Sonoma 14 or later.

Move IFFViewer to Applications and open it once. Finder will then show thumbnails for
IFF, ILBM, LBM, and PBM images, and Spacebar opens a full Quick Look preview.

## Quarantine Flag

Development builds are not notarized and may be marked as coming from an unidentified
developer. You can remove the quarantine flag from a build you trust:

**Terminal**:
just run this command `xattr -rc ~/Downloads/IFFViewer.app`

**GUI**: Sentil is a great app for managing the quarantine and signing. Free to use. It's [here](https://github.com/alienator88/Sentinel).

**System Settings**: Open Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Enjoy.
