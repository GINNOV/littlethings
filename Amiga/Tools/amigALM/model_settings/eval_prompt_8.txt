### INSTRUCTION ###
Generate a highly efficient, relocatable Amiga assembly subroutine to perform the following task.

### TASK ###
wait_for_left_mouse_click

### SPECIFICATION ###
- **Inputs**: None.
- **Outputs**: None. The routine should loop until a left mouse click is detected and then return.
- **Register Preservation**: All registers from D3-D7 and A2-A6 MUST be preserved.
- **Scratch Registers**: D0, D1, D2, A0, and A1 can be freely used.
- **AmigaOS Calls**: None. Direct hardware polling is required.
- **Format**: Standard subroutine ending with an RTS instruction.
- **Comments**: Comment on the polling of the CIAAPRA register (port A of CIA chip A) and testing bit 6 for the fire button/left mouse button.
