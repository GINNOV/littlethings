### INSTRUCTION ###
Generate a highly efficient, relocatable Amiga assembly subroutine to perform the following task.

### TASK ###
draw_filled_box_with_blitter

### SPECIFICATION ###
- **Inputs**: A1 should be a pointer to the screen bitmap. D0.w should be the x,y starting coordinates (x in high byte, y in low byte). D1.w should be the width,height (width in high byte, height in low byte). D2.w should be the color index (0-7).
- **Outputs**: None.
- **Register Preservation**: All registers from D3-D7 and A2-A6 MUST be preserved.
- **Scratch Registers**: D0-D2, A0-A1 can be freely used.
- **AmigaOS Calls**: The routine must wait for the blitter to be free before and after the operation.
- **Format**: Standard subroutine ending with an RTS instruction.
- **Comments**: Comment on the setup of BLTCON0, BLTCDAT, BLTADAT, and BLTSIZE.
