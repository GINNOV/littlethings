## Retrieving Your Flume API Secrets <a id="retrieving-your-flume-api-secrets" />
To connect this dashboard to your Flume data, you need four pieces of information: your Flume **Username**, **Password**, **Client ID**, and **Client Secret**.

### Steps to get Client ID and Client Secret:
1. **Log in to the Flume Portal**: Go to [portal.flumewater.com](https://portal.flumewater.com) and log in with your standard Flume account.
2. **Access Settings**: Click the **Settings** (gear icon) in the left-hand navigation menu.
3. **API Access**: Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page to find the **API Access** section.
4. **Generate Credentials**: Click the **"Generate API Client"** button.
5. **Copy the Keys**: Your **Client ID** and **Client Secret** will be displayed. Copy these immediately and store them securely.

> **Security Note**: Treat your Client ID and Client Secret like a password. Do not share them or commit them to public repositories.

## Configuration <a id="configuration" />
Once you have your secrets, you need to provide them to the application.

### Local Development
Create a file named `.env.local` in the root of the project and add the following:

```env
FLUME_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id_here
FLUME_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret_here
FLUME_USERNAME=your_flume_email@example.com
FLUME_PASSWORD=your_flume_password_here
```

### Production (Vercel)
If you are deploying to Vercel, add these same keys as **Environment Variables** in your project settings on the Vercel Dashboard.

## Using the Dashboard <a id="using-the-dashboard" />

### Home Dashboard
The home page provides a real-time (or near real-time) snapshot of your water usage.
- **Current Flow**: See if water is currently running (GPM).
- **Daily Usage**: A bar chart showing usage over the last 14 days.
- **Hourly Breakdown**: A line chart showing usage over the last 24-48 hours.

### Historical Explorer
Navigate to `/history` to dive deep into your data.
- **Date Range**: Select custom start and end dates.
- **Granularity**: View data by minute, hour, day, or month.
- **Comparison**: Compare usage across different periods.

### Insights & Alerts
The `/insights` page helps you understand your consumption patterns.
- **Alerts**: View recent leak detections or high-usage notifications.
- **Anomalies**: Automatically identifies days where usage was significantly higher than average.
- **Stats**: View your average daily use and peak consumption times.

### Settings
Manage your dashboard preferences in `/settings`.
- **Units**: Toggle between Gallons and Liters.
- **Budgets**: View your current budget vs. actual usage.
- **Devices**: If you have multiple Flume devices, you can switch between them here.

## Troubleshooting <a id="troubleshooting" />
- **No Data Appearing**: Ensure your `.env.local` variables are correct and that your Flume device is online.
- **Rate Limits**: The Flume API has a limit of 120 requests per hour. If charts stop loading, you may have hit this limit. Wait a few minutes and try again.
- **Authentication Error**: Double-check your username and password. If you recently changed your password in the Flume app, you must update it in your environment variables.
