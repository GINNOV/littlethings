# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-18

First release of Joy2, a macOS Lab pad that drives a HUENIT arm from a Speedlink Competition Pro Extra.

- Hold the stick to keep moving in X/Y; hold left fire for Z and cup angle
- Right fire toggles suction (`M1400` via Joy1)
- Pad lights the cells the stick is using
- Cheatsheet dialog with a labeled photo of the stick
- Connect first; pad and module controls stay hidden until the arm is linked
- STOP, Esc, or leaving the window ends motion
- Does not send `G28`
