<p>
  The Commodore Amiga is a legendary platform that revolutionized
  computing with its advanced graphics, sound, and multitasking. This
  site is dedicated to “almost” developers who share a passion for
  coding on the Amiga, whether in 68k assembly, C, or modern AmigaOS with
  macOS in mind.</p>
<p class="mt-4">
  By “almost” I mean you know your way around coding tools, but you can't
  split the byte just yet like a digital lumberjack.
</p>
<p class="mt-4">
  My fascination with the Amiga began in the 1980s, drawn to its
  groundbreaking hardware and vibrant developer community. From coding
  demos in AMOS to exploring AmigaOS, the Amiga shaped my love for
  programming. 
  </p>
  <p class="mt-4">
  Back then I only spoke my native language and there was
  no Internet. Even BBS were out of my reach. 40 years later and a
  successful career that would have not been possible if it wasn’t for
  the Amiga in my life, I have a hitch on "what I could have learned if I
  didn’t have the knowledge gap?"
  </p>
  <p class="mt-4">
  So... let’s find out together. I will share
  what I learn and if you know better share away.
</p>
<p class="mt-4">
  There are many resources out there, consider this my open learning
  diary back to when I was 15 with some additional powers 😅. <br>Aloha 🌴.
</p>
